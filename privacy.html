Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026.

Machine is a private, single-user personal assistant (see the home page). Its only user is its owner.

What Machine accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, Machine may access the owner's own Gmail messages, Google Calendar events, Google Contacts, and Google Drive files, Docs and Sheets, within the permissions shown on Google's consent screen.

How the data is used

The data is used only for tasks the owner asks for: reading and summarising messages, drafting and sending email on the owner's behalf, keeping the calendar, and working with the owner's documents.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with other people. To produce replies, Machine sends the content a task needs to the AI model providers the owner uses (currently Anthropic and OpenAI), which process it on the owner's behalf.

Storage and retention

Data is stored only on infrastructure the owner controls, and the owner can delete it at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue.
