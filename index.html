Machine

Machine is a private, single-user personal assistant. One person operates it for their own work. It is not offered to the public: there is no sign-up, there are no other users and nothing is for sale.

With the owner's consent, Machine connects to the owner's own Google accounts to read, organise and draft the owner's email, calendar, contacts and documents (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets). It never asks for access to anyone else's account.
